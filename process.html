How We Work

A seamless, concierge-level experience from first conversation to final installation.

01

Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with an in-depth conversation. We take the time to understand your vision, style preferences, and lifestyle needs — so every decision that follows is rooted in what matters to you.

02

Design Concept

Based on our consultation, we present a curated design concept that reflects your vision — from furniture selections and finishes to spatial flow and styling direction.

03

Ordering & Sourcing

We take full charge of sourcing and ordering every item required for your project. You approve, we execute — with access to vendors and pieces you won't find on your own.

04

Tracking & Logistics

Our team manages all logistics end-to-end, ensuring everything arrives on time and in perfect condition. No coordinating deliveries, no chasing vendors — we handle it all.

05

Installation & Styling

We handle the installation and final styling of your space. When we're done, your home is ready for you to walk in and enjoy — no assembly, no decisions left to make.
